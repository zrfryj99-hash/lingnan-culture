01 / ABOUT LINGNAN

Discover Lingnan

A distinctive landscape shapes a distinctive spirit. Centuries of exchange have left a rich cultural legacy.

Lingnan Heritage

Where Mountains Meet the Sea

Lingnan broadly refers to the lands south of the Five Ridges in southern China. Its cultural landscape is especially associated with Guangdong, Guangxi and Hainan. A warm, humid climate, an extensive coastline and a network of rivers have shaped a way of life that is open, practical, expressive and inventive.

From the ancient Nanyue kingdom to the Maritime Silk Road, and from Cantonese and Chaoshan communities to Hakka traditions, different peoples have met and influenced one another here. Lingnan culture is rooted in history while remaining open to change.

05 / THROUGH TIME

Through Time · 文脉绵延

Far from being isolated, Lingnan has long connected inland China with the sea, and local traditions with the wider world.

Pre-Qin to Han

Baiyue Peoples & Nanyue

Local communities developed diverse traditions. Administrative changes and the Nanyue kingdom helped deepen connections between the Central Plains and Lingnan.

Tang & Song Dynasties

Trade & Maritime Exchange

Trade through Guangzhou flourished. Maritime routes brought people, goods and ideas, enriching the city and its culture.

Ming & Qing Dynasties

Growing Ports & Overseas Communities

Cantonese, Chaoshan and Hakka cultures continued to develop. Overseas trade and migration shaped distinctive hometowns with strong links to communities abroad.

Modern Era

A Spirit of Innovation

岭南成为近代思想、教育、实业与革命活动的重要区域，Openness创新的精神延续至今。

In Lingnan today, historic neighbourhoods stand alongside modern cities. Living heritage enters schools and communities, old buildings find new life, and traditional crafts meet contemporary design. Culture is not simply preserved in museums—it continues to grow through everyday life.
